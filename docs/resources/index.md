# Learn & Practise

Tools tell you **what you can use**. Learning resources teach you **what to understand and how to practise**. This directory is a first-class part of the guide, not an afterthought.

## Resource types

- **Courses:** structured lessons with a sequence and learning outcomes.
- **Websites and platforms:** guided learning environments and reference portals.
- **YouTube channels and playlists:** supplementary explanations and demonstrations.
- **Official documentation:** the source of truth for installation, options, and supported behavior.
- **Labs and CTFs:** controlled places to practise.
- **Books and standards:** deeper explanations and authoritative reference material.
- **Communities:** places to ask questions and learn with others; these will be reviewed for authenticity and safety.

## How we evaluate a resource

A listing should describe its subject, level, prerequisites, format, language where known, access or cost caveats, and why it is recommended. We link to the original provider instead of copying its lessons.

We distinguish between:
- **Link checked:** the destination was opened.
- **Content reviewed:** a contributor inspected the learning material and confirmed that the description is fair.
- **Practice tested:** a contributor completed the stated exercise or checked the lab instructions.

Prices, free tiers, account requirements, and course availability can change. Always check the provider's current terms before planning around a resource.

Use [the resource-entry template](../templates/resource-entry.md) when suggesting another course, channel, website, or lab.
