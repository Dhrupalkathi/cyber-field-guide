# CTFs & Labs

This page lists general challenge platforms and event directories. Subject-specific platforms have one canonical home in their relevant resource page; this page links to them instead of repeating their entries.

A CTF (Capture the Flag) is a structured challenge in an environment designed for authorized learning. A platform's beginner label does not mean every challenge is suitable for every learner.

## General challenge platforms

### picoCTF

> **CINS stage fit:** Stage 1 onward — optional beginner-friendly challenges; choose topics that match your current foundations  
> **Level:** Beginner to intermediate, depending on challenge · **Type:** CTF and learning platform

Provides introductory challenges across several cybersecurity and computing categories.

- **Best for:** Exploring problem-solving challenges after learning the relevant underlying concepts.
- **Official resource:** [picoCTF](https://picoctf.org/)
- **Review state:** Official platform link checked; challenge availability may vary by event.

### Hacker101 CTF

> **CINS stage fit:** After Stage 2 — web-security learning  
> **Level:** Beginner to intermediate · **Type:** Web-security course and CTF

HackerOne describes Hacker101 as a free class with video lessons and CTF challenges.

- **Prerequisites:** Basic HTTP and web-application concepts are helpful before attempting web challenges.
- **Official course:** [Hacker101](https://www.hacker101.com/)
- **CTF platform:** [Hacker101 CTF](https://ctf.hacker101.com/)
- **Review state:** Official Hacker101 pages checked 31 October 2026.

### Hack The Box Academy

> **CINS stage fit:** Module-dependent — check each module's prerequisites; use after the relevant CINS foundations  
> **Level:** Beginner to advanced, depending on module · **Type:** Guided learning modules and paths

Provides structured technical modules with explanations and interactive learning environments.

- **Access:** Some Tier 0 modules are free; other modules and paths may require Cubes or a subscription. Confirm each module's current access.
- **Module example:** [Getting Started](https://academy.hackthebox.com/course/preview/getting-started)
- **Catalogue:** [Academy paths](https://academy.hackthebox.com/catalogue/paths)
- **Review state:** Platform help and catalogue pages checked 31 October 2026.

## Find competitions

### CTFtime

> **CINS stage fit:** All stages — optional event discovery; choose events by their published rules and difficulty  
> **Level:** Varies by event · **Type:** CTF calendar

Lists scheduled team and individual CTF competitions.

- **Official resource:** [CTFtime](https://ctftime.org/)
- **Safety:** Check event rules, organizer identity, permitted targets, and participation requirements before joining. An event listing is not proof that every event is suitable for beginners.
- **Review state:** Calendar destination linked; individual events must be checked separately.

## Safety before joining a lab or competition

- Read the current platform or event rules and scope.
- Stay within the named lab or authorized target environment.
- Do not scan, attack, or pivot to unrelated systems.
- Do not publish challenge flags, paid walkthroughs, or other learners' private data.
- Keep personal credentials and private target data out of public write-ups.

Use the [CINS Safety and Ethics rules](../club/safety-and-ethics.md) whenever the permitted scope is unclear.
