# YouTube Channels & Video Learning

Video channels can make difficult topics easier to visualize, but individual videos can become outdated. Use them as supplements to structured learning and official documentation—not as the only source for commands, product behavior, or security decisions.

Each channel is listed here once. Subject pages link back to this directory instead of repeating channel descriptions.

## How video recommendations are reviewed

A specific video or playlist recommendation should identify:
- the exact video or playlist, not just the channel;
- the topic and intended level;
- publication date when version-sensitive;
- whether commands or product screens may be outdated;
- the official documentation a learner should cross-check.

Do not blindly copy terminal commands from a video. Inspect what a command does, where it connects, what files it changes, and whether the activity is authorized. Be cautious with unofficial downloads, "one-click" scripts, affiliate-linked tools, and guaranteed-job claims.
