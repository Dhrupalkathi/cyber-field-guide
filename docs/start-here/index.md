# Start Here

## Welcome, beginner

This guide assumes you may be completely new to cybersecurity. You may not know what Kali Linux is, what a port means, or why people use a terminal. That is fine.

**You do not need Kali Linux to start.** First understand the field, learn the safety boundaries, and build a mental model of computers. Specialist security tools come later.

## Use AI to explore topics you choose

At the beginning of each stage, you will find instructions for using an AI assistant as a personal tutor. Ask it for explanations, worked examples, fictional scenarios, guided simulations, code walkthroughs, or an interactive discussion about any topic you want to understand better.

Verify important information with official sources. Do not share passwords, tokens, private records, or confidential material with AI tools. Follow the authorization and safety rules in the curriculum.

When you understand a topic, you may write your own notes, diagrams, explanations, or resource page and submit it to the CINS repository. A human review for accuracy, safety, privacy, and source attribution is required before publication. Do not publish raw, unverified AI output.

## What should you install?

Nothing specialist for Stage 0. Stage 1 can be learned with an ordinary computer and a browser. The Python track can begin in a browser-based environment; local Python setup is optional and should follow the official installation guidance.

Do not install Kali, a large SIEM, or a collection of penetration-testing tools just because a video recommends them.

## Safety boundaries

- Do not scan your college network, public IP ranges, or websites without explicit authorization.
- Do not run commands copied from videos or AI tools without understanding them and checking reliable documentation.
- Do not disable security controls on your everyday laptop to make a tool work.
- Do not upload passwords, API tokens, session cookies, personal data, or private logs to this repository.
- Do not submit credentials, recovery codes, or personal account screenshots.

When you are ready, continue to [Learning Resources](../resources/index.md) and later choose a specialization through [Learning Paths](../learning-paths/index.md).
