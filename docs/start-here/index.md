# Start Here

## Welcome, beginner

This guide assumes you may be completely new to cybersecurity. You may not know what Kali Linux is, what a port means, or why people use a terminal. That is fine.

**You do not need Kali Linux to start.** First understand the field, learn safe practice, and build a mental model of computers. Specialist security tools come later.

## What should you install?

Nothing specialist for Stage 0. Stage 1 can be completed with an ordinary computer and a browser. The Python track can begin in a browser-based environment; local Python setup is optional and should follow the official installation guidance.

Do not install Kali, a large SIEM, or a collection of penetration-testing tools just because a video recommends them.

## Safe first activities

- Identify the assets in a fictional online account or college-portal scenario.
- Decide whether a proposed security exercise has clear authorization and scope.
- Draw a simple computer diagram showing CPU, RAM, storage, input, and output.
- Read a sample file's properties without opening unknown files.
- Write a small Python program and explain how it works.
- Record a troubleshooting hypothesis, test, result, and next step.

## What not to do

- Do not scan your college network, public IP ranges, or websites without explicit authorization.
- Do not run commands copied from videos or repositories without understanding them.
- Do not disable security controls on your everyday laptop to make a tool work.
- Do not upload passwords, API tokens, session cookies, personal data, or private logs to this repository.
- Do not submit credentials, recovery codes, or personal account screenshots as proof of completing a personal-security checklist.

## Learn, practise, and show your work

Use the [Stage 0/1 resource directory](../resources/stage-0-and-1.md) to choose a small number of suitable materials. For each module, make notes, complete the practice activity, and pass the checkpoint. A certificate is not a substitute for demonstrating understanding.

When you are ready, continue to [Learn & Practise](../resources/index.md) and later choose a specialization through [Learning Paths](../learning-paths/index.md).
