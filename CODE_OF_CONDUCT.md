# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, constructive, and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or viewpoint.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while discussing technical disagreements.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Welcome beginner questions and explain unfamiliar terms.
- Give actionable feedback and acknowledge contributions.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's personal information.
- Keep security learning ethical and within explicitly authorized scope.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption, trolling, or repeated bad-faith conduct.
- Encouraging unauthorized access, harm, or disclosure of secrets in project spaces.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions that violate this Code of Conduct and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Decisions should be proportionate, documented, and applied consistently.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to a project maintainer through the contact route shown on the repository's GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive details in public issues. If no private contact route is available, ask a maintainer for one without publishing the report itself.

## Scope

This Code applies in project issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces where someone represents the project.
